      The rules underneath

      Claude reads and proposes

      • Classifies each attachment, and the body when it carries accounting content.
      • Proposes vendor, accounts, tax codes and tags, using only ids from the QuickBooks cache.
      • Gets a second, independent reading when the vendor is new, has no history, or a hold tag is present.
      • Turns a free-text reply such as "code it to Legal Fees" into one structured change.

      Code decides

      • Byte-identical files are caught by SHA-256 and never reach the model.
      • Postable documents from unknown senders go to a person.
      • Anything the model skipped still gets a record and a question.
      • A refusal or disagreement holds the item. A changed answer is validated again.

      Hard lines

      • No payments, and no payee or bank-detail changes.
      • Never posts into a period before the QuickBooks closing date.
      • Kill switch, and a daily cap of 25 entries or $50,000 for rule-approved posting.
      • Every entry carries its Bob proposal id and can be reversed by Bob.
